Ham and Cheese Toastie

Ham and Cheese Toasties ready to eat

A simple meal, that never ceases to delight.

Always handy to have available in a pinch.

Ingredients

  • loaf of bread
  • sliced Ham
  • chese slices (tasty is best)
  • butter
  • salt
  • pepper

Steps

  1. butter both sides of the bread
  2. place cheese slices on each piece of bread
  3. place ham on one side
  4. garnish with salt and pepper
  5. place two halves together
  6. butter top of the bread
  7. place toastie butter side down onto sandwich press
  8. butter the un buttered top
  9. close lid and await the 2 minutes
