Spaghetti Bolognese

A plate of Spaghetti Bolognese

A wonderfully warm dish that suits all seasons.

Perfect on a cold day, just be prepared to wait a while for it to cook!

Ingredients

  • 500g of Beef and/or Pork mince
  • 1 onion
  • garlic clove x 2
  • 1 can of diced or crushed tomatos
  • 1 sachet of tomato paste
  • 1 beef stock cube
  • salt
  • Oliver Oil
  • 1 pack of spaghetti

Steps

  1. Peel onion
  2. Dice onion
  3. Heat up saucer
  4. once hot, add the onions and the garlic with a dash of olive oil
  5. stir until onions have softened
  6. add minced meat to the saucer
  7. stir meat until fully brown
  8. add can of tomato and stir it through
  9. add tomato paste whilst stiring
  10. add in stock cube
  11. stir until uniform in colour
  12. turn heat down to low and place the lid on top of saucer
  13. periodically check and stir whilst simmering
  14. add salt as necessary during frequent taste tests
  15. when nearly ready (20 minutes or so away) fill another pot with water and put to the boil
  16. add salt to the boiling water
  17. once boiled, add pasta to the water
  18. maintain close watch as pasta cooks, testing frequently
  19. once done, empty water and begin to plate up
