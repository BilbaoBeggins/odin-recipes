Hot Dogs

A lovely pair of HotDogs, ready to be eaten

Another quick and easy favourite.

The flavour is all up to how many toppings you apply!

Ingredients

  • hot dog buns
  • hot dogs
  • mustard
  • tomato sauce

Steps

  1. cook hotdogs in boiling water
  2. place cooked hot dogs into bun
  3. generously apply mustard and tomato sauce
  4. enjoy!
